This will confirm our understanding reached during
recent negotiations regarding the necessity to expeditiously
staff vacant, newly created, or temporary situations.

As agreed, in the event a need should arise to fill any
such situations at Edison, The Times and the Union agree, on an
inverse seniority basis, to assign the most junior qualified
situation holder to that situation pending final award or, in the
case of temporary situations, termination of the situation.

Junior situation holders assigned in accordance with
the above shall be given no less than four (4) days’ notice prior
to such assignment. At the conclusion of a temporary situation,
they shall be restored to their former situation. They shall be
permanently assigned to permanent or vacant positions in the
event those situations are not filled by bid.

Prior to instituting the procedure above, The Times
shall notify the Chapel Chairman of the vacancy. The Chapel
Chairman will, within three (3) days of such notification, supply
the name of a situation holder who wishes to voluntarily accept
the situation at Edison to The Times. Such situation holders
shall fill the situation within seven (7) days from the date of
the above notification to the Chapel Chairman. In the event the
situation is not filled by bid, the most junior qualified
situation holder will be permanently assigned to the vacancy and
the volunteer shall return to his regular situation.

The above procedures shall not be applied to any

temporary vacancy which is foreseen to be of two (2) weeks or
less duration.
