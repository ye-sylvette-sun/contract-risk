






                                 SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT

                This Agreement is by and between Argus Research Group, Inc., a corporation
incorporated under the laws of Delaware with offices at 61 Broadway, New York, New York,
and Petroleum Argus Limited, a corporation organized under the laws of the United Kingdom
with offices at 93 Shepperton Road, London NI 3DF, England.

                                    Background to Agreement

                Argus Research Group, Inc. has requested timely extensions of time to oppose the
application filed by Petroleum Argus Limited to register the mark PETROLEUM ARGUS in the
United States Patent and Trademark Office in International Classes 9, 16 and 36 (App.
No. 75/337,886) as published in the United States Patent and Trademark Office on
November 14, 2000.

                The parties have agreed to resolve this potential dispute in accordance with the
terms set forth below:

                                       Terms of Agreement

1.     Petroleum Argus Limited shall amend the identification of goods and services set forth in
the application to register PETROLEUM ARGUS (App. No. 75/337,886) to the following:

       Electronic publications, namely, journals, newsletters, reports, newspapers, pamphlets,
       periodicals, magazines, and manuals reporting oil pricing worldwide, developments in
       the oil industry, and related topics such as oil drilling, technology and infrastructure and
       not including information concerning asset management and investment advice relating
       to stocks and bonds in electronic format downloadable from and made available via a
       global computer network on a subscription basis.

       Printed publications, namely, journals, newsletters, reports, newspapers, pamphlets,
       periodicals, magazines, and manuals reporting oil pricing, developments in the oil
       industry, and related topics such as oil drilling, technology and infrastructure and not
       including information concerning asset management and investment advice relating to
       stocks and bonds, in Class 16.

       Financial information services regarding the energy industry in the nature of conveying
       pricing information about fuel and energy products via a global computer network and
       not including information concerning asset management and investment advice relating
       to stocks and bonds, in Class 36.

2.      Argus Research Group, Inc. will not oppose or seek to cancel the trademark application
for PETROLEUM ARGUS (SN 75/337,886) owned by Petroleum Argus Ltd. or otherwise
contest or challenge Petroleum Argus’ rights in the mark PETROLEUM ARGUS as shown in
that application for the goods and services as set forth in that application as amended pursuant to

therein or do any act or thing in derogation thereof.

3.     Argus Research Group, Inc. will not oppose or seek to cancel the trademark registration

4.     This agreement and the performance of shall be governed by and be construed under the
laws of the State of New York where the agreement was prepared without recourse to New
York’s conflicts of law principles. This provision extends to the United States, its territories and
possessions.

5.      This agreement is binding upon and inures to the benefit of the parties, their successors,
licensees and assigns.

6.      This is the entire agreement and may be modified only in writing signed by both parties.

7.      Both parties will pay all their own costs in attorneys’ fees.

8.    The parties will agree to keep the terms of this agreement confidential except as required
by Court order and/or state and federal rules of civil procedure.
