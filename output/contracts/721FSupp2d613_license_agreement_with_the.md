LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made by and between the CITY OF YOUNGSTOWN, OHIO
(hereinafter the "City") and HARDRIVES PAVING & CONSTRUCTION, INC.
(hereinafter “Hardrives”), an Ohio corporation.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual representations, warranties, and
covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. The City agrees to grant to Hardrives a license for use of the property, including,
but not limited io, fixtures, existing improvements, and appurtenant structures thereon,
all located within the City and described more fully in the attached Exhibit A (hereinafter
the "Premises").

2. Use. The City and Hardrives agree that the Premises will be declared surplus
property and deemed not needed for public use by the Director of Finance and is to be
used in connection with economic development purposes. Hardrives intends to utilize
the Premises for the production, distribution, and sale of paving materials and other
similar products and for the operation of its management and corporate offices. The
Premises will further be used for the storage and operation of all equipment, personal
property, and supplies necessary for said purposes.

3. Rental. Hardrives agrees to pay to the City a rental fee of one dollar ($1.00) per
year. |
4, Event of Default. Failure to make annual payments within thirty (80) days of the

date payment is due shall constitute an event of default. Upon written notice by the
City, Hardrives shall have ten (10) days, within which to cure such default. If said
default is not cured within that ten (10) day period: following notice from the City,
Hardrives shall be held to have breached this Agreement and shall surrender all rights
to the use of the Premises.

5. Terms. This Agreement shall continue: (1) for a term of one (1) year from the
date of execution of this Agreement; or (2) until the date that title to the Premises is
conveyed and transferred to Hardrives by the City, pursuant to a Development
Agreement entered into by and between the City and Hardrives, regarding the
Premises, which shall be entered into on or before December 31, 1999.

6. Representation and Warranties of the City. The City represents and warrants to
Hardrives, as follows:

SECTION IX
SUMMARY OF FINDINGS - PHASE II ESA

This summary reflects an examination and assessment of the Phase I ESA
findings, subsequent geophysical monitoring, field activities, analytical
results, and comparison of applicable federal and state regulations and
standards. Field activities were conducted on August 10 to 31, 1992. Based upon
the information collected herein, presented as a part of the Phase II ESA, the
following findings are offered for properties proposed for acquisition by the
City of Youngstown from LTV Steel Corp., and further identified as LTV Steel Co.
Industrial Development Site, Parcels 1-2-3. The site collectively comprises
123.07 acres and is situated within the City of Youngstown, Mahoning County,
Ohio.

J. GENERAL

1. The summary of findings of the Phase I ESA has identified that the
subject property is part of a larger complex identified as LTV Steel
Corp., 1290 Poland Avenue, Youngstown, Ohio. The overall site,
including the assessment study area, appears on U.S. EPA’s,
RCRA/CERCLA List.

2. Portions of the assessment study area are known to have contained
RCRA waste management units, storage areas, and other areas where
wastes and products currently regulated under RCRA, CERCLA or ISCA
were generated, treated, stored or disposed. Information from
federal, state, and local regulatory agencies have indicated that
RCRA regulated portions of the assessment study area were closed in
1985 to 1988 in accordance with Ohio EPA and U.S. EPA protocols.
‘Historical chemical handling, including waste disposal, warranted
further investigation (Phase II ESA) for portions of all three

parcels.
3. Groundwater and surface water flows were determined to be in a north

to northeasterly direction towards the Mahoning River for Parcels 1.
