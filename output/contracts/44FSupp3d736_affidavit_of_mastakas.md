### ASSIGNMENT

For valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, it is hereby agreed that:

1. Sandoz Inc. ("ASSIGNOR") assigns to Exel, Inc. ("ASSIGNEE") any and all actions, causes of action, claims, and demands of every kind and nature whatsoever, in law or in equity, which ASSIGNOR may have, or reasonably claim to have, against Southern Refrigerated Transport, Inc., its parents, subsidiaries, affiliates, predecessors in interest, successors in interest, insurers, and directors, officers, employees, agents, counselors and assigns of each of them ("CARRIER"), based in whole or in part upon the loss/shortage to cargo identified in Sandoz Inc. invoices with reference numbers 94147255, 94146099, 94146100, 94146101, 94146102, transported by CARRIER under bills of lading with reference numbers 0081338275, 0081338158, 0081338274, 0081338276 and 0081338281, dated November 7, 2008, from Memphis, Tennessee to Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania ("the Cargo").

2. ASSIGNOR warrants and represents that at all times relevant it has held and maintained the sole and exclusive rights and interest in the Cargo.

3. The construction and effect of this Assignment shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York without giving effect to its conflict of laws principles. The Parties acknowledge that those executing this Assignment on their behalf are authorized representatives acting within the scope of their authority. This Assignment may be executed in two or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed to be an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
