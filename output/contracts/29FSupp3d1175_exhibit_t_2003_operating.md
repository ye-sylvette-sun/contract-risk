## INTRODUCTION

The Meriter Health Services Employee Retirement Plan (the "Plan") represents an amendment and restatement of the Madison General Hospital Pension Plan. It is the Employer's intention that the Plan, as set forth herein, shall comply with the provisions of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, the Tax Equity and Fiscal Responsibility Act of 1982, the Tax Reform Act of 1984, the Retirement Equity Act of 1984, and the Tax Reform Act of 1986, including corresponding and other applicable provisions of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended from time to time.

The Plan, as amended and restated effective as of January 1, 2003, or such other dates as are stated herein or as are required by law, reflects the applicable requirements of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (Uruguay Round Agreements Act), the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act of 1994, the Small Business Job Protection Act of 1996, the Taxpayer Relief Act of 1997, the Internal Revenue Service Restructuring and Reform Act of 1998, and the Community Renewal Tax Relief Act of 2000 (collectively referred to as "GUST") and the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Act of 2001 ("EGTRRA").

The Plan is intended to be a qualified defined benefit plan under Code section 401(a).

## ARTICLE 2ELIGIBILITY AND PARTICIPATION

2.1 Eligibility. Each Employee in the employ of the Employer on the Effective Date who was a Participant in the Plan shall be eligible to participate in this Plan on the Effective Date. All other Employees who have attained age 21 shall be eligible to participate as of the earlier of the April 1 or October 1 (the "Participation Date") following the anniversary of the first day of an Eligibility Computation Period in which the Employee is credited with at least 1,000 Hours of Service and provided:

(A) He is not a leased employee who may be excluded under Section 414(n) of the Code, or

(B) He is not covered by the terms of a collective bargaining agreement between the Employer and a bargaining representative of such person, unless such collective bargaining agreement specifically provides that he is eligible to participate in this Plan.

Employees who met the eligibility requirements of the Prior Plan but who chose not to contribute to the Prior Plan, and as a result were not Prior Plan Participants, become Plan Participants effective January 1, 2003. In the event that any questions arise as to the eligibility to participate in the Plan, they shall be decided in accordance with the provisions of Article 8.

2.2 Leave of Absence. Each Employee who, at the time he would otherwise have become a Participant, shall be absent from employment as a result of service in the Armed Forces or is on leave duly granted by the Employer or on lay-off for lack of work, shall become a Participant as of the Participation Date coincident with or

next following the date on which such Employee shall have resumed his active Employment.

2.3 Reemployment. A Participant who separates from service and is reemployed by the Employer, who had a vested benefit derived from Employer Contributions prior to incurring a Break in Service, or the number of whose consecutive one year Breaks in Service is less than the greater of five or the number of years of Eligibility Service which he had completed prior to his Break in Service, shall be eligible to participate in the Plan immediately upon his return to the employ of the Employer. A Participant who separates from service with the Employer and is reemployed by the Employer, who had no vested benefit derived from Employer Contributions prior to incurring a Break in Service, and the number of whose consecutive one year Breaks in Service is greater than the greater of five or the number of years of Eligibility Service which he had completed prior to his Break in Service, shall be considered a new Employee for eligibility purposes.

2.4 Services With Another Employer. The Employer may grant past service credit to an Employee for employment with another employer performed prior to commencement of the Employee's employment for the Employer. Such past service credit may be given for purposes of determining the Employee's Eligibility Service and/or Vesting Service under the Plan. The granting of such past service credit is subject to action by the Plan Administrator to specify the prior employer and the purposes for which such service shall be credited. All employees of the prior employer shall be treated uniformly, and the granting of such past service credit shall be done in a manner that does not discriminate in favor of officers, shareholders, or highly compensated employees.

### 13.3

- (A) In each Plan Year in which the Plan is a Top-Heavy Plan, each Participant who is a Non-Key Employee (except a Participant who is a Non-Key Employee as to the Plan Year of reference but who was a Key Employee as to any earlier Plan Year) who has been credited with 1,000 or more Hours of Service during such Plan Year will receive a minimum Accrued Benefit.   - (2) thirty percent (30%) of the Participant's Top-Heavy Compensation.

The minimum accrual applies even though under other Plan provisions the Participant would not otherwise be entitled to receive an accrual, or would have received a lesser accrual for the year because (i) the Non-Key Employee fails to make mandatory contributions to the Plan, (ii) the Non-Key Employee's Compensation is less than a stated amount, (iii) the Non-Key Employee is not employed on the last day of the accrual computation period or (iv) the Plan is integrated with Social Security.

- (B) If a Non-Key Employee described in Subsection (A) participates in both a defined benefit plan and a defined contribution plan described in Section 13.2(A)(1) and (2), the Employer is not required to provide such Non-Key Employee with both the minimum benefit under this Plan and the minimum contribution under the defined contribution plan. In such event, the Non-Key Employee shall receive the minimum benefit described in this section, offset by the benefit provided by the defined contribution plan.

- (C) The Minimum Accrued Benefit required (to the extent required to be nonforfeitable under Section 416(b) of the Code) may not be forfeited under Sections 411(a)(3)(B) or 411(a)(3)(D) of the Code.

#### Vesting

- (A) Change in Schedule. Each Participant's vested interest in his Accrued Benefit shall be determined in accordance with the following schedule for any Plan Year in which the Plan is a Top-Heavy Plan, unless the vesting schedule previously set forth in the Plan provides more rapid vesting for such Participant:

| Years of Service |             | Percent Vested |
|------------------|-------------|----------------|
| less than        | two years   | 0%             |
|                  | two years   | 20%            |
|                  | three years | 40%            |
|                  | four years  | 60%            |
|                  | five years  | 80%            |
|                  | six years   | 100%           |

- (B) Shift Out of Top-Heavy Status. If a Top-Heavy Plan ceases to be a Top-Heavy Plan, the vesting schedule previously set forth in the Plan shall again apply to all years of service. However, a Participant shall maintain the same vested interest in his Accrued Benefit determined under the schedule in Subsection (A) as of the date on which the Plan ceases to be a Top-Heavy Plan until the Participant's vested percentage under the schedule previously set forth in the Plan exceeds the percentage maintained under the schedule in Subsection (A).
- (C) Special Election of Vesting Schedule. Each Participant with at least five years of service at the time that the Plan ceases to be a Top-Heavy Plan may elect to continue to have his vested percentage computed under the Plan in accordance with the vesting schedule set forth in Subsection (A). The period during which the election may be made shall commence on the date on which the Participant is informed that the Plan is no longer a Top-Heavy Plan and shall end 60 days thereafter.

13.5

Compensation. If the Plan becomes a Top-Heavy Plan, the Annual Earnings of any Participant for purposes of accruing a benefit under the Plan for that Plan Year and all prior Plan Years of participation shall not exceed \$200,000 (or such greater amount as may be prescribed by applicable governmental regulations). However,

this will not result in the reduction of any Participant's Accrued Benefit determined as of the last day of the Plan Year before the Plan becomes a Top-Heavy Plan.

In the case of a Plan Year of duration shorter than 12 months in which the Plan is a Top-Heavy Plan, Annual Earnings shall exclude total taxable income of any Participant in excess of the amount determined by multiplying \$200,000 (as adjusted) by a fraction, the numerator of which is the number of full calendar months occurring in such short Plan Year and the denominator of which is 12.

- 13.6 Social Security. For each Plan Year in which it is a Top-Heavy Plan the Plan must meet the requirements of this article without regard to any Social Security or similar contributions or benefits.
- 13.7 Adjustment to Maximum Benefit Limitation. For each Plan Year in which the Plan is a Super Top-Heavy Plan, the 1.25 factor in the defined benefit and defined contribution fractions described in the "Maximum Benefit" provisions of the Plan shall be reduced to 1.0. The adjustment described in this subsection shall not apply to a Participant during any period in which the Participant earns no additional accrued benefit under any defined benefit plan and has no employer contributions, forfeitures, or voluntary nondeductible contributions allocated to his accounts under any defined contribution plan.
- 13.8 Suspension of Benefits. Notwithstanding the other provisions of the Plan, the payment of a Participant's Accrued Benefit shall not be suspended during the Participant's reemployment during any period in which the Plan is a Top-Heavy Plan.
