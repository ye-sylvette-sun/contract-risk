         AMENDED AND RESTATED SETTLEMENT, AMENDMENT, AND RELEASE
                               AGREEMENT

           This AMENDED AND RESTATED SETTLEMENT, AMENDMENT, AND RELEASE
   AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) dated as of May 19, 2016, is entered into by and among
   Aéropostale, Inc., Aéropostale Procurement Company, Inc. and their affiliated chapter 11 debtors
                                               1
   and debtors in possession (the “Debtors”) , and MGF Sourcing US, LLC (f/k/a TSAM
   (Delaware) LLC) (“MGF”). This Agreement amends, restates and supersedes in its entirety that
   certain Settlement, Amendment, and Release Agreement dated as of May 16, 2016 among the
   undersigned parties.

           WHEREAS Aéropostale, Inc., Aéropostale Procurement Company, Inc. and MGF are
   each party to that certain Sourcing Agreement dated as of May 23, 2014 (as amended through the
   date hereof, the “Sourcing Agreement”);

           WHEREAS the Debtors filed voluntary petitions for relief under chapter 11 of title 11 of
   the United States Code on May 4, 2016, in jointly administered cases captioned In re
   Aéropostale, Inc., et al., Case No. 16-11275 (SHL) pending before the United States Bankruptcy
   Court for the Southern District of New York (the “Bankruptcy Court);

           WHEREAS the Debtors and MGF have various disputes and have asserted various
   claims against one another, including in connection with the Sourcing Agreement; and

           WHEREAS the parties hereto have agreed to settle their disputes and claims (to the
   extent set forth herein) and amend the Sourcing Agreement (to the extent set forth herein), in each
   case, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

           NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements
   contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

   1.        Deliveries and Delivery Dates.

            (a)     MGF shall deliver the goods identified on the Detailed Delivery Schedule (the
   “Goods”) in compliance with the delivery schedule set forth in the Detailed Delivery Schedule, or
   earlier as described below. “Detailed Delivery Schedule” means the summary sheet and excel
   support provided to the Debtors by MGF on May 9, 2016, which is attached hereto as Exhibit A.
   For purposes of this Agreement, the terms “Open Commitments,” “Open CPOs”, “Open Orders”,
   “US Released for Delivery” and “Orders in Transit” are references to categories of Goods as
   identified on the Detailed Delivery Schedule.

            (b)     MGF shall accelerate deliveries of Goods (or change shipping method from
   ocean freight to another method designated by the Debtors) where possible at the Debtors’ written
   election and at Debtors’ sole cost and sole risk of any delivery delays occasioned as result thereof
   (it being agreed that any re-direction of Goods from Debtors’ Canadian warehouse to the United
   States warehouses of the Debtors shall also be at Debtors’ sole cost and sole risk of any delivery

            (c)     The Debtors shall be obligated to pay for all Goods that are delivered at full

   less than two weeks consistent with past practice).

           2.       Inventory. The Debtors shall take delivery of all Goods with no reductions and
   no cherry-picking (subject to the provisions of the Sourcing Agreement that provide for
   adjustments for defective product and similar adjustments, including without limitation non-
   conforming deliveries, excluding any right to reject late deliveries as non-conforming deliveries
   unless any such late delivery is ten (10) days or more past the scheduled delivery date set forth on
   the Detailed Delivery Schedule). If MGF attempts delivery of Goods from Open Orders earlier
   than indicated on the Excel Support, the Debtors shall accept delivery of such Goods when
   delivery is attempted by MGF.

            3.     Trade Terms. The Debtors shall pay for the Goods delivered by MGF in full no
   later than fourteen (14) calendar days after delivery; provided that the Debtors shall pay for
   Goods to be delivered by MGF in full on a cash in advance basis (with respect to each specific
   individual delivery of Goods, no later than two business days prior to the delivery of any such
   Goods) after an Event of Default (as defined in the DIP Financing) has occurred under the
   Debtors’ current Secured Superpriority Debtor in Possession Loan, Security and Guaranty
   Agreement, the Interim Order Pursuant to 11 U.S.C. §§105, 361, 362, 363 and 364 and Rules
   2002, 4001 and 9014 of the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (i) Authorizing Incurrence by
   the Debtors of Postpetition Secured Indebtedness, (ii) Granting Liens, (iii) Authorizing Use of
   Cash Collateral by the Debtors and Providing for Adequate Protection, (iv) Modifying the
   Automatic Stay, and (v) Scheduling a Final Hearing, entered on May 6, 2016, or any final order
   referenced therein (collectively, the “DIP Financing”), unless such Event of Default was (a)
   caused by or related to any breach by MGF of the terms of this Agreement and/or the Sourcing
   Agreement, as modified by this Agreement; (b) cured by the Debtors in accordance with the
   terms of the DIP Financing or waived by the administrative agent under the DIP Financing within
   ten (10) calendar days of the occurrence of such Event of Default; or (c) caused by MGF’s
   acceleration of the Detailed Delivery Schedule (other than at the request of the Debtors). All
   payments referenced herein shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the
   account designated by MGF in writing on or prior to the date hereof unless and until MGF
   changes such account designation in writing.

             4.       Run-Off License. In the event of a liquidation of all or substantially all of the
   Debtors’ retail stores and/or the merchandise held for sale therein (a “Full Chain Liquidation”)
   approved by the Bankruptcy Court, beginning as of the commencement date of such Full Chain
   Liquidation as set forth in an agency agreement governing such Full Chain Liquidation (such
   date, the “Commencement Date”) and ending on the later of (x) December 31, 2017 and (y) one
   year after the commencement of such Full Chain Liquidation, the Debtors hereby grant MGF a
   worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid up, irrevocable run-off license to all of the Debtors’
   trademarks and other intellectual property of any kind sufficient to permit MGF to sell Goods not
   yet delivered to the Debtors (but only to the extent that title to such Goods has not passed to the
   Debtors), subject to the following: (a) the run-off license shall not permit MGF (or its agents or
   representatives) to sell Goods in the United States during the sixty (60) day period immediately
   following the Commencement Date (it being agreed that in the event any such Full Chain
   Liquidation is stayed by the Bankruptcy Court for any reason, such sixty (60) day period shall be
   extended day-for-day concurrently with the period of such stay), and (b) if MGF wants to sell
   Goods to a third party in any country (other than the United States) in which the Debtors
   currently (as of the date hereof) authorize the sale of Aéropostale products to any licensee, then in
   such country MGF may only sell such Goods to such licensee. Subject to the foregoing
   limitations, the run-off license shall provide that, effective as of the commencement of such
   license as described above, MGF shall be entitled to complete the manufacture of, sell or
   otherwise dispose of all or any of the Goods not yet delivered to the Debtors (but only to the
   extent that title to such Goods has not passed to the Debtors) and make any advertisement or
   other promotion in furtherance thereof on a worldwide basis through one or more sales channels
   as MGF deems appropriate in it sole discretion (it being agreed and understood that no such
   advertisement or promotion shall be commenced until such run-off license becomes effective; for
   the avoidance of doubt, there shall be no advertising or promotion in the United States (or relating
   to sales of Goods to occur in the United States) prior to the date on which MGF is permitted to
   sell such Goods in the United States as set forth in clause (a) of the preceding sentence); provided
   that the run-off license shall be expressly limited to the foregoing and shall be valid for no other
   purpose. Notwithstanding the foregoing, MGF shall not be entitled to such license if the Court
   denies the proposed DIP Financing on a final basis based on the objection of Aero Investors LLC,
   MGF Sourcing Holdings, Limited (f/k/a TSAM Holdings, Limited) or Sycamore Partners or any
   of its affiliates.

            6.      Sourcing Agreement Termination. The Debtors shall not submit any new orders
   to MGF under the Sourcing Agreement, and the Debtors shall notify their international licensees
   that they will not be permitted to submit any new orders to MGF. After MGF makes the last
   delivery of Goods set forth on the Detailed Delivery Schedule and the Debtors make the final
   payment for Goods delivered by MGF, in each case as contemplated by this Agreement, the
   Sourcing Agreement shall automatically terminate and be of no further force or effect. The
   parties hereto each acknowledge and agree that the termination fee, the shortfall payment, and the
   rebate provisions of the Sourcing Agreement shall be of no further force or effect as of the date
   that the Settlement Order (as defined hereinafter) becomes a final order. MGF further agrees not
   to make any claim related to the termination fee and the shortfall payment under the Sourcing
   Agreement and the Debtors agree to waive any claim for any rebate under the Sourcing
   Agreement.

           7.       Bankruptcy Court Approval/Effectiveness of Agreement. The parties to this
   Agreement shall comply with the terms and conditions of this Agreement pending approval of
   this Agreement by the Bankruptcy Court, and MGF shall continue to take all actions necessary to
   comply with its production and delivery obligations for all of the Goods. The Debtors shall file a
   revised version of Exhibit A to the motion to approve this Agreement pursuant to Rule 9019 of
   the Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure (the “Settlement Motion”) on May 20, 2016, and shall
   seek the Settlement Motion’s approval by the Bankruptcy Court on May 23, 2016. If the Debtors
   do not file the Settlement Motion and seek Bankruptcy Court approval thereof in accordance with
   the terms of this Agreement, or if the Bankruptcy Court either denies the Settlement Motion or
   fails to enter an order approving the Settlement Motion in form and substance reasonably
   acceptable to MGF (the “Settlement Order”) on or before May 30, 2016, then this Agreement
   shall be of no further force or effect; provided that the Debtors’ obligation to pay for any Goods
   delivered prior thereto and MGF’s obligation to provide 14 day payment terms for any such
   Goods delivered prior thereto shall survive until fully performed.
