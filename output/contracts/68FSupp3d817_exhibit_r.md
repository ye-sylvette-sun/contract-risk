# AGREEMENT

This Agreement is by and between Diamond Aviation LLC with address at: 6068 Highway 98 West, (suite 1), Box 311 Hattiesburg, MS 39402 herein known as "DA" and RizoJet Aviation Services, LLC with address at: 508 Vandenberg Street, Hondo, TX 78861 herein known as "BROKER".

Whereas DA has provided an Client, (Dr. Lynn McMahan), herein known as "CUSTOMER" whom desires to purchase a Citation aircraft, N60FJ, serial number 551-0007, ("Aircraft") from BROKER plus obtain assistance in the acquisition of said aircraft; and whereas BROKER is in the aviation business and will be providing services on behalf of CUSTOMER and DA to identify, locate, & negotiate either the lease or purchase, it is hereby agreed as follows:

## GENERAL

BROKER is working with DA to assist in possible negotiations with CUSTOMER. DA at all times presents the CUSTOMER with the best available information and does not represent other parties that could be counter to CUSTOMER'S best interests, either financially or operationally. BROKER agrees to pay DA a finders fee/commission for services rendered as set forth in this agreement and aircraft purchase agreements relevant to N60FJ, SN 551-0007.

## COMMISSION

BROKER'S and DA'S commission/finder's fee described in this agreement is subject to the following restrictions-Commission/finder's fee is subject to the following disbursement schedule as contained in attached Exhibit A. The Commission Schedule is contingent of sale of said Aircraft and is due upon closing and reception of funds to Aero-Space Reports ("Escrow Agent") for sale of said Aircraft whose primary business is the handling of aircraft related escrow funds.

## FUNDS

All funds exchanged per this AGREEMENT will be in U.S. Dollars and will be drawn upon a domestic U.S. bank or a recognized federally approved financial institution. All funds shall be made payable in accordance with attached Exhibit A.

# OTHER

BROKER and DA shall have no authority to bind either party, ("Diamond Aviation LLC and RizoJet Aviation Services, LLC) except upon written order or authorization by both parties. It is further understood that both parties shall at all times during the continuance of this Agreement use its best efforts to protect the interests of both parties in the event of claims by and on behalf of, or against, either party.

The relationship hereby created is not exclusive and that both parties hereby certifies that it has no ownership, financial interest in, or control of the agencies or other parties from which products or services are procured under.
