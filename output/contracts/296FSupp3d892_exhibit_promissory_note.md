# **PROMISSORY NOTE**

\$100,000.00

Nashville, Tennessee  
April 16, 2010

This note replaces a previous note dated January 15, 2010, in the amount of Fifty Thousand Dollars (\$50,000.00), in order to reflect a total amount to become outstanding of One Hundred Thousand Dollars (\$100,000.00).

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, the undersigned, PATRICIA PORTER KRYDER (the Maker), promises to pay to the order of JAMES KEMMLER ROGERS, (together with any other lawful owners and Holders of this Note being herein collectively referred to as the "Holder"), the principal sum of One Hundred Thousand Dollars (\$100,000.00) in legal tender of the United States, together with interest thereon as follows:

from the date of draw through the date of repayment, interest at four percent (4.00%) per annum on the outstanding unpaid principal balance until such principal is paid. Interest shall be payable monthly in arrears commencing thirty days after the date of first draw at a place designated by the Holder. All principal and unpaid interest shall be payable at a place designated by the Holder, on or before December 31, 2020

Maker may prepay the principal hereof, in whole or in part, without penalty, at any time, and interest shall be due on all amounts so prepaid to the date of prepayment. All such prepayments shall be applied first against the interest and then against principal.

This note is to be secured in the amount of the principal and interest by a Deed of Trust on part of the same property conveyed to Maker by deed of record at Book 246, Page 760 in the Register's Office of Giles County, Tennessee, Map 29, Parcel 10.01.

The Maker agrees that if this Note is placed in the hands of an attorney for collection or attempting to collect the indebtedness evidenced hereby, the Maker shall pay, on demand, to the Holder hereof its reasonable attorney's fees, together with all court costs and other expenses paid by such Holder.

This Note may not be assigned, transferred, or sold by Maker without the express prior written consent of Holder, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

This Note shall be governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee.
