RECITALS :

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Seller’s Purchase, Warranties and Servicing Agreement,
dated as of October 18, 2004, between EMC and GreenPoint Mortgage Funding, Inc.
(“GreenPoint”), as amended by Amendment Number One dated as of September 20, 2006 and
the term sheets dated September 27, 2006 and September 28, 2006 (collectively, the “Purchase
Agreement”), EMC purchased or received from GreenPoint certain home equity revolving credit
loans (the “HELOCs”); .

WHEREAS, EMC has sold and assigned its entire interest in the HELOCs to the
Depositor, and the Depositor has accepted from EMC the sale and assignment of such interest in
the HELOCs pursuant to the Mortgage Loan Purchase Agreement, dated as of March 6, 2007 by
and between EMC and the Depositor (the “Depositor Purchase Agreement”);

WHEREAS, a Trust Agreement, dated as of February 13, 2007, by and between the
Depositor and the Owner Trustee, as amended by the Amended and Restated Trust Agreement,
dated as of March 6, 2007, among the Depositor, the Owner Trustee and the Securities
Administrator (as may be further amended, modified or supplemented from time to time as set
forth therein, the “Trust Agreement”) provides for, among other things the formation of
GreenPoint Mortgage Funding Trust 2007-HE1 (the “Issuer” or the “Frust”) and the issuance of
the GreenPoint Mortgage Funding Trust 2007-HE1 Certificates (the “Certificates”’);

WHEREAS, a Seller’s Purchase, Warranties and Servicing Agreement, dated as of
October 18, 2004, between EMC and GreenPoint as amended by Amendment Number One dated
as of September 20, 2006 (collectively, the “Servicing Agreement”) provides for, among other
things, the servicing of the HELOCs by the Servicer;

WHEREAS, a Sale and Servicing Agreement, dated as of March 6, 2007, among the
Depositor, the Issuer, the Indenture Trustee, the Master Servicer as Master Servicer and
Securities Administrator, and the Sponsor as seller (as may be amended, modified or
supplemented from time to time as set forth therein, the “Sale and Servicing Agreement”)
provides for, among other things, the sale of the HELOCs by the Depositor to the Trust and the
master servicing of the HELOCs by the Master Servicer;

WHEREAS, an Indenture, dated as of March 6, 2007, by and between the Trust, the
Securities Administrator and the Indenture Trustee (as may be amended, modified or
supplemented from time to time as set forth therein, the “Indenture”) provides for, among other
