DECLARATION OF TRUST

WELLS FARGO TRUST FOR SECURITIES LENDING

This Declaration of Trust, made on / Oo} 24) OO , 2000, by Wells Fargo
Bank Minnesota, N.A. as Trustee, creates a Maryland business trust for the
investment and reinvestment of money and property received by the Trust from
time to time. The Trustee declares that all money and property received by the
Trust shail be held and managed in trust pursuant to this Declaration of Trust. The
name of the Trust created by this Declaration is the Wells Fargo Trust for Securities
Lending (the “Trust”),

RECITALS

{a} The Trustee desires to establish a Maryland business trust pursuant to
- the Maryland Business Trust Act of the Annotated Code of Maryland (Corporations
and Associations, Title 12 Business Trusts) for the investment and reinvestment of
money and other property contributed thereto by participants in the securities
lending program administered by the Trustee or its affiliates.

(b) The Trustee intends that the Trust hereby created be exempt from
registration under the Investment Company Act of 1940 pursuant to the qualified
purchaser exemption contained in Section 3(c)(7).

fc) The Trustee further intends that the Trust created hereby not be
considered a “publicly traded partnership” for federal income tax purposes and to
that end commits to limiting participation in the Trust to less than 100
Shareholders. The Trustees further intend that the Trust not be subject to taxation
in the State of Maryland.
