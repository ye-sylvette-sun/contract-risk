I. SUMMARY

The Wells Fargo Trust for Securities Lending (the “Trust”) Is offering shares of
beneficial interest In the Wells Fargo Collateral Investment for Term Loans Trust
Fund (the “CI Term Trust”) to qualified institutional purchasers. The Trust was
established pursuant to a Declaration of Trust dated October 24, 2000 and as further
amended from time to time (the “Declaration of Trust”). The purpose of the Trust is
to provide lenders engaged In securities loan transactions (entered into through
Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. as their lending agent) a continuous source of managed
investment for cash collateral and for other qualified investors with investments

objectives substantially similar to those for investment of securities lending cash
collateral.

The Trust is a Maryland business trust organized under the Annotated Code of
Maryland Corporations and Associations, Title 12 Business Trusts. Wells Fargo Bank,
N.A. (Wells Fargo”) currently serves as the sole trustee. Subscribers will become
beneficial owners (“Shareholders”) of interests in the CI Term Trust, a specific Series
of the Trust, and will be entitled to share in the Income and gains and losses of that
Series upon acceptance of their subscriptions by Wells Fargo and payment of the
consideration specified by Wells Fargo from time to time.

Yi. THE TRUSTEE

Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. is a national banking association organized under the laws of
the United States with principal offices in Minneapolis, Minnesota and Columbia,
Maryland and is a wholly owned subsidiary of Wells Fargo & Company. As of

V. INVESTMENT OBJECTIVES AND POLICIES

Investment Objectives

The Cl Term Trust seeks to achieve a positive return compared to the daily Fed
Funds rate by investing in high-quality United States dollar-denominated securities
where the prime considerations for such investments are safety of principal and daily
liquidity requirements. The Cl Term Trust may Invest in securities with fixed,
variable, or floating rates of Interest. Subject always to the limits on portfolio
maturity described below, it is anticlpated that the securities purchased for the
portfolio may be matched in maturity and/or index to the maturity and/or index of
the securities loans negotiated by Wells Fargo on behalf of the Subscribers.

The CI Term Trust will endeavor to maintain a stable $10.00 price per Share,

although no assurance can be given that it will achieve its investment objective or
maintain a stable Share value.

VI. RISK FACTORS

The value of the Shares will change with the change in value of the CI Term
Trust’s investments. Many factors can affect those values,

Because the CI Term Trust Invests in debt securities, such as notes and bonds,
the CI Term Trust is subject to credit risk, interest rate risk, and liquidity risk,

Credit risk is the risk that the issuer or guarantor of a debt security or the
counterparty to a derivative or repurchase agreement is unable or unwilling to
make timely principal and/or interest payments or to otherwise honor its
obligations, in which case, the value of the related debt security may decline.
Debt securities are consequently subject to varying degrees of credit risk,
which is often reflected in the credit rating given to a particular security.

Interest rate risk is the risk that Interest rates may Increase, which would
reduce the resale value of the securities held by the CI Term Trust (including
any investments in U.S. Government obligations). Those debt securities with
longer durations tend to be more sensitive to changes In interest rates, usually
making them more volatile in value than securities with shorter durations, In
addition, changes in market interest rates may also extend or shorten the
