### **2. Royalties, Quantity and Payments**

(a) The royalty rate is equal to \$0.87 per sold unit of the Product sold by Licensee or its manufacturer (the "Royalty Rate").

(b) Licensee projects to manufacture at least 225,000 units of the Product in the First Year, and 262,500 units of the Product in the Second Year, and 325,000 units of the Product in the Third Year.

(c) Licensee agrees to notify Licensors in quarterly intervals the number of Products sold for the prior quarter.

(d) Licensee shall pay Licensors within 10 days after the end of the previous month the following: \$16,313 each month for each of the 12 months of the First Year, \$19,031 each month for each the 12 months of the Second Year, and \$23,562 each month for each of the 12 months of the Third Year.

(e) At the end of each of the First Year, Second Year, and Third Year, Licensee shall pay Licensors an Excess Payment computed as the Royalty Rate multiplied by the number of Products sold in that year in excess of the projected amount for that year set forth in section (b) of this Section. For example, if, in the First Year, 230,000 Products were sold then at the end of the First Year, then the Licensee will make an Excess Payment of \$4,350 to Licensors.

(f) At such point in time when at least \$706,875 in the aggregate has been paid to Licensors by Licensee in accordance with this Section, then Sections (d) and (e) of this Section are no longer operative and instead Licensee will pay Licensors the Royalty Rate for each subsequent unit of the Product sold [in excess of aggregate 812,500 units for which payment had already been made] in quarterly intervals until the Termination Date.

(g) Past due amounts of royalties more than 30 days past due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is lower.

 





(h) Notwithstanding anything herein to the contrary, the terms "sale", "sold", "sell", "purchase", "purchased", or similar words, when used in connection with a Product, shall mean any sale, conveyance, gift, assignment, or other transfer of any kind, whether with or without consideration, to any person or entity whatsoever.

(j) Notwithstanding anything herein to the contrary, in the event of termination of this Agreement and within thirty (30) days thereafter, Licensee shall pay to Licenser royalties at the Royalty Rate based upon all units of the Product purchased or ordered by Licensee, or otherwise in its possession and control that has not been included in previous royalty payments to licenser.
