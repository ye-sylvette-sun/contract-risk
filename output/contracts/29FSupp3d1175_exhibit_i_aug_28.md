### JOINT RESOLUTION OF THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEES OF MERITER HEALTH SERVICES, INC. AND MERITER RETIREMENT SERVICES, INC.

RE: Modified Financial Model for MainGate Development Project

WHEREAS, in September 2001, the MHS and MRS Boards of Directors established threshold goals for fundraising and apartment home pre-sales which were to be met prior to proceeding with the ground breaking for MainGate at Meriter Commons, and in January 2002 the Boards approved a plan of financing for the project; and

WHEREAS, due primarily to the downturn in the economy, those threshold goals were not achieved by the May 1, 2002, target date, with the Capital Campaign having raised \$3,802,508 toward the \$4,000,000 threshold goal and the Sales Team having pre-sold 32% of the life lease dollar value as compared to the 50% sales target; and

WHEREAS, upon further study of the market assumptions and forecast and further analysis of projections for fund raising and sales, management has determined that the project continues to be a financially viable, significant component of Meriter's senior housing development strategy; and

WHEREAS, various financing scenarios which would allow construction to begin prior to achieving the threshold goals and which result in acceptable financial ratios and financial performance were reviewed by the MRS Executive and Finance Committees on June 4, 2002; and

WHEREAS, management and the MRS Executive and Finance Committees also reviewed the risks of delaying the project, concluding that delay of MainGate past September 2002 will have negative implications for current donors, committed prospective residents, future pre-sales, project costs and project credibility, and also concluding that proceeding with construction will create a positive stimulus for additional donations and apartment sales; and

WHEREAS, management has prepared a revised business model, financing plan and financial proforma for consideration by the Boards of Directors of Meriter Health Services and Meriter Retirement Services; and

WHEREAS, the MHS Board of Directors has authorized the MHS Executive Committee to make decisions necessary to resolve any policy issues related to the MainGate project on behalf of the MHS Board of Directors and to approve a modified business model acceptable to both Meriter Health Services and Meriter Retirement Services;

- continued -

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the MHS and MRS Executive Committees hereby recommend for approval by the MRS Board of Directors the modified financial model for MainGate at Meriter Commons, with a revised total project cost not to exceed \$19,023,125; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Executive Committees recommend that the MRS Board authorize proceeding with construction no later than September 15, 2002, with the understanding that this date may be prior to achieving the fund raising and pre-sales targets originally established for the project; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the life lease contracts be changed to defer collection of 85% of the fees until the residential units are ready for occupancy, recognizing that this creates the need for a construction loan; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the MHS and MRS Executive Committees recommend approval of the plan to obtain a \$7 million construction loan by issuing a two-year taxable variable rate note with USBank on terms substantially the same as those described in the materials submitted to the Committees as of this date, and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Committees recommend the Meriter Foundation Board endorse the possibility of placing restrictions on the freedom to use Foundation funds designated for Meriter Retirement Services during the period of time the construction note is outstanding in order for MRS to preserve a minimum liquidity balance of \$5 million, the sum of which includes a combination of MRS cash, marketable securities and funds held by the Foundation ; and

FURTHER RESOLVED, that in accordance with the authority granted to it in the resolution adopted by the MHS Board of Directors on June 26, 2002, the MHS Executive Committee hereby approves the modified financial model for MainGate and the plan of action to implement that model as described by the foregoing resolutions, and further authorizes and directs management to take any and all necessary steps and to execute any documents necessary to complete that plan of action, including obtaining the construction loan, with the understanding that this action of the MHS Executive Committee shall be reported to the MHS Board of Directors in August 2002.

ADOPTED this 11th day of July, 2002.
