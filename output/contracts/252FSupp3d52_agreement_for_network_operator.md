                           ARTICLE 1. PURPOSE OF AGREEMENT

           1.1    Purpose of Agreement

                 1.1.1   MTC is building the MassBroadband 123 Network, defined herein as the

         MTC Network, a broadband network currently planned to have fiber in approximately

         124 communities in western and north central Massachusetts and initially connect

         approximately 1,392 community anchor institutions that serve them, all as more fully

         defined herein.

                 1.1.2   Network Operator is a provider of network operations and support services

         and solutions and is experienced and skilled in the administration, management,

         maintenance, provisioning, performance and sale of third party access to complex

         network systems such as the MTC Network.

                 1.1.3   MTC is in need of a provider of network operation and support services,

         as more specifically defined herein, and Network Operator desires to provide such

         services to MTC, which will include, among other things, providing advice and

         recommendations to MTC regarding the selection of the appropriate network design,


#10069598_v13                                    1




         fiber, equipment, and configuration; participating in acceptance testing of the MTC

         Network; operating and managing the MTC Network; facilitating the Interconnection of

         and service to Wholesale Customers; constructing and administering procedures for

         others to construct Network Extensions on the MTC Network; and upgrading and

         updating the MTC Network electronics and other equipment to keep the MTC Network

         operating efficiently and accommodating increasing bandwidth demands.

                 1.1.4   MTC desires that these services be rendered hereto by Network Operator

         and that the functions, responsibilities and tasks as described in this Agreement and

         otherwise commonly performed by a dark fiber and lit network provider be performed

         and assumed by Network Operator in accordance with this Agreement.
