VI. RISK FACTORS

The value of the Shares witl change with the change in value of the Trust's
investments. Many factors can affect those values,

Because the Trust Invests in debt securities, such as notes and bonds, the
Trust Is subject to credit risk and interest rate risk. If the issuer or guarantor
of a debt security or the counterparty to a derivative or repurchase agreement
is unable or unwilling to make timely principal and/or Interest payments or to
otherwise honor its obligations, the value of the related debt security may
decline. Debt securities are consequently subject to varying degrees of credit
risk, which Is often reflected In the credit rating given to a particular security.

interest rate risk is the risk that Interest rates may increase, which would
reduce the resale value of the securitles held by the Trust (Including any
Investments In U.S, Government obligations). Those debt securities with
longer durations tend to be more sensitive to changes In Interest rates, usually
making them more volatile in value than securities with shorter durations. In
