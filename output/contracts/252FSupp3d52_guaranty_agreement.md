GUARANTY AGREEMENT —

THIS AGREEMENT is made this 25 day of February, 2011, by and between Axia
NetMedia Corporation, a corporation organized under the laws of the Province of Alberta,
Canada (‘Guarantor’) and Massachusetts Technology Park Corporation, an independent public
_ instrumentality of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, established, organized, and existing
pursuant to Chapter 40] of the Massachusetts General Laws and doing business as the
Massachusetts Technology Collaborative, together with its successors and assigns ("MTC"),

WHEREAS, MTC and Axia NGNetworks USA, Inc., a Delaware company registered in
Massachusetts and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Guarantor (“Network Operator”), are considering
entering into a Master Agreement for a network operator (the "Network Operator Agreement"),
pursuant-to which Network Operator would agree to perform certain network operator services and
to undertake certain financial, operational and other responsibilities relating to the MassBroadband
123 fiber optic network MTC is planning to constract, all as more specifically defined in the
Network Operator Agreement; and

WHEREAS, in order to enhance the security sought by MTC of the Network Operator,
Guarantor agrees to issue this guarantee for the direct benefit of MTC as provided herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the promises herein
contained, Guarantor does hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE I
COVENANTS AND AGREEMENTS

2.1 | Guarantor does hereby guarantee to MTC and its successors and assigns that, should
Network Operator default in any of its payment or performance obligations under the Network
Operator Agreement, Guarantor, on behalf of Network Operator, (a) shall make all such payments
and perform all such obligations of the Network Operator, as described in and in accordance with
the terms of the Network Operator Agreement, and as such obligations may be changed in
accordance with the terms of the Network Operator Agreement (the "Guaranteed Obligations"); and
(b) shall fully and punctually pay and discharge, as the same become due and payable, any and all
costs, expenses and liabilities for or in connection with the Guaranteed Obligations, including, but
not limited to, the costs of causing the substituted performance of the Guaranteed Obligations. This
guatanty is limited to and capped at the amount of Four Million ($4,000,000) US Dollars, and


should Guarantor advance to MTC funds up to said amount, Guarantor shall have no further
obligation or liability under this Agreement,

2.2 This Agreement shall be a continuing, absolute and unconditional (except as noted
in section 2.1 above) guaranty and shall remain in full force and effect until all obligations of
Network Operator under the Network Operator Agreement have been fulfilled, irrespective of the
bankruptcy, insolvency, merger, reorganization, termination, ‘discontinuance or dissolution of
Network Operator, or any assignment for the benofit of creditors by Network Operator.

2.3 This Agreement and the liability hereunder shall not be affected or impaired by any
compromise, settlement, release, renewal, extension, indulgence, change in or modification of any
of the obligations and liabilities of Network Operator under the Network Operator Agreement, or
by any failure on the part of MTC, its successors or assigns, to realize upon any obligations or
liabilities of Network Operator.

2.4 Without limiting the scope of this Agreement as set forth above, The obligations,
covenants, agreements and duties of the Guarantor under this Agreement shall not be affected or
impaired by reason of the happening fiom time to time of any of the following with respect to the
Network Operator Agreement or this Agreement: .

A, The waiver by MTC of the performance or observance by Network
Operator, Guarantor of any of the agreements, covenants, terms or coniditions contained in
the Network Operator Agreement or other documents executed in connection therewith;

B. The extension of the time for the performance by the Network Operator of
any obligations under the Network Operator Agreement or of any other obligations under or
arising out of any the Network Operator Agreement or the extension or the renewal of the
same; or |

C, The modification or amendment (whether material or otherwise) of any duty,
agreement or obligation of Network Operator set forth in the Network Operator Agreement,

2.5 In an Event of Default (as defined below) MTC, or its successors and assigns, shall
have the right to proceed directly against the Guarantor. Notwithstanding any other provision of
this Guaranty or the Network Operator Agreement, in the event that a default of Network Operator
occurs and is continuing under the Network Operator Agreement, Guarantor shall have the right to ’
cure such default within the time period, if any, provided Network Operator in the Network
Operator Agreement. In the event that Guarantor cures such default within such period, MTC shall
accept such cure by Guarantor as if Network Operator had performed same,

. ARTICLE Ill
DEFAULTS AND REMEDIES

3,1 No remedy herein conferred or reserved is intended to be exclusive of any other
available remedy or remedies, if any, but each and every such remedy shall be cumulative and shall
be in addition to every other remedy given under this Agreement or now or hereafter existing at law
or in equity or by statute. No delay or omission to exercise any right or power accruing upon any
default, omission or failure of performance hereunder shall impair any such right or power or shall
be construed to be a waiver thereof, but any such right and power may be exercised from time to
time and as often as may be deemed expedient, In the event any provision contained in this
Agreement should be breached by any party and thereafter duly waived by the other party so
empowered to act, such waiver shall be limited to the particular breach so waived and shall not be
deemed to be a waiver of any other breach hereunder,

3.2 The occurrence of one or more of the following shall constitute an Event of Default
and entitle MTC to the remedies provided herein:

A, The Network Operator fails to cure any default under the Network Operator
Agreement within the time for cure specified therein, or if no cure period is specified, then
within thirty (30) days after receiving notice of default from MTC to Network Operator
under the Network Operator Agreement;

B, Network Operator is adjudicated a bankrupt, or a trustee or receiver is
appointed for Network Operator for all or a substantial part of its property by an involuntary
proceeding, or Network Operator files a petition in bankruptcy or an answer not denying
jurisdiction in bankruptcy, or if Network Operator makes an assignment for the benefit of
creditors, or if Network Operator becomes insolvent.
