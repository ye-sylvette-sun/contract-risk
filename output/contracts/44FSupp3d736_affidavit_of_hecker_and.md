## MASTER TRANSPORTATION SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT, made this 26 day of Dec., 2007, by and between Southern Refrigerated Transport Arkansas corporation ("Carrier") and EXEL INC., a Massachusetts corporation ("Customer").

WHEREAS, Carrier represents that it is engaged in the business of transporting property by motor vehicle as a contract carrier in domestic (interstate and intrastate) and foreign commerce, it is duly and legally registered and/or licensed, as applicable, as a Carrier at the federal, state, Provincial or Canadian levels, as applicable, which enables it to lawfully engage in its business, and it desires to provide such services to Customer for the transportation of general commodities ("Commodities"); and

WHEREAS, Customer arranges for the transportation of Commodities on behalf of the underlying shipper(s) (Customer's principal(s)) of the Commodities ("Shipper") and desires to use Carrier to provide transportation services designed to meet the specialized transportation requirements and needs of Customer and/or Shipper (the "Services").

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions hereinafter set forth, Carrier and Customer agree as follows:

## 1. TERM, RENEWAL AND TERMINATION

This Agreement shall be effective upon its execution and shall continue for a period of one year from the date of execution or unless earlier terminated by either party, with or without cause, upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party. After the first year, this Agreement shall be extended on a Year-to-year basis unless terminated by either party upon thirty- (30) day's prior written notice to the other party. If at the time of termination, Carrier is in possession of a shipment, Carrier shall ensure it completes its delivery commitment to Customer and no such termination shall release either party from any obligation accruing before such termination or, except as otherwise provided for in this Agreement, preclude either party from exercising any remedies to enforce such obligations.

## 2. AGREEMENT NON-EXCLUSIVE

The parties agree that the Services provided under the terms of this Agreement will be on a non-exclusive basis. Customer is free to use the services of any other Carrier or may itself perform the Services. Carrier may perform transportation services to others.

## 3. SCOPE OF WORK

Carrier agrees to accept for arrangement of shipment Commodities moving in interprovincial, intraprovincial, interstate, intrastate or foreign commerce, as applicable, for transportation to the destinations designated by Customer in such a manner as to satisfy the specialized needs of Customer and/or Shipper. This Agreement applies to mixed and unmixed transportation of regulated and unregulated Commodities, including hazardous materials and beverages alcoholic in nature.

Subject to and upon the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the Exhibits and Schedules attached thereto, Broker agrees to provide the Services, including those set forth on Exhibit A and the following:

- a) Carrier shall, at its own cost and expense, ensure that it maintains suitable equipment (including all fuel, oil, tires and other supplies) and personnel capable of performing the Services under this Agreement in a satisfactory and legal manner, and to obtain and keep all licenses and permits necessary to perform the Services, and to comply with all federal,

- state and local laws or Canadian, Provincial and local laws, as applicable and regulations pertaining to the performance of the Services.
- b) Equipment must be clean and in good appearance, insect and rodent free, odor free and water tight and uncontaminated by the prior use of the equipment for transportation of toxic chemicals, including pesticides, rodenticides, insecticides and/or other hazardous materials, substances or chemicals. Trailers shall not otherwise be used for transportation of any liquid or solid waste or garbage. Carrier shall not commingle any Commodities tendered by Customer with freight from another shipper which contains toxic chemicals, including pesticides, rodenticides, insecticides and/or any other hazardous materials, substances or chemicals. Trailers failing any of the above shall be rejected at no cost to Customer. Failure by Customer to reject any trailers and the acceptance or loading of a trailer by Customer shall not relieve Carrier of any liability hereunder;
  - c) For TL movements, unless otherwise directed by Customer, Carrier shall not, without Customer's prior written consent, commingle any Commodities tendered by Customer with the freight of another shipper or consignor;
  - d) Qualified, licensed and experienced drivers shall be used in providing the Services;
  - e) Special services such as, picking up and delivering freight on short notice, weekend services, twenty-four hour services, holiday service and other similar services shall be provided;
  - f) At Customer's request, Carrier will stop in transit to partially load or unload freight;
  - h) Appropriate load protection devices shall be provided by Carrier and used to secure the Commodities in the trailer;
  - i) Carrier must have the desire and capability to pursue EDI opportunities upon terms and conditions acceptable to Customer; and
  - j) Carrier's performance under this Agreement shall be evaluated as designated by the Customer's representative and may be modified from time to time during the term hereof.
  - k) There shall be no charge assessed by Carrier for any costs or expenses involved in arranging or fulfilling an appointment with respect to pick up and delivery services to consignee or consignor.
  - l) The term "pick up" as used herein, means the service performed by Carrier, or its agent, in calling for freight at Exel Inc.'s door or dock. Customer's employees will load the freight.
  - m) The term "delivery" as used herein means the service performed by Carrier or its agent in transporting freight to consignee's door or job site.

## 4. RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING

Customer shall issue and Carrier shall sign freight receipts for each shipment in the form acceptable to Customer and the Shipper. If a bill of lading is used as a freight receipt, any terms, conditions or provisions thereof shall be subject and subordinate to the terms of this Agreement and, in the event of a conflict, this Agreement shall govern. Carrier's or Carrier's agent signature on the receipt or bill of lading shall be prima facie evidence that the Commodities were received in good condition unless otherwise noted on the face of such document. Upon delivery of each shipment, Carrier or Carrier's agent shall obtain a delivery receipt from the consignee(s), in form required by the Shipper, showing the Commodities delivered, their condition at time of delivery and the date and time of delivery.

## 5. DELIVERY ARRANGEMENTS; DELAYS; RECONSIGNMENT

- a) Unless otherwise directed by Customer, Carrier shall contact final consignee and all stop-off consignees included in a scheduled shipment to make arrangements, prior to Carrier accepting legal responsibility for the shipment, for appointments necessary to assure the timely delivery and unloading of Commodities. Carrier shall notify Customer of such appointments prior to Carrier moving the trailer from the pickup location.
- b) If Carrier encounters pickup or delivery problems that prevent the timely pickup or delivery of all or any part of a shipment, Carrier shall immediately notify Customer by telephone or facsimile for resolution and further instructions.
- c) Carrier shall not re-consign any shipment or any scheduled stop off delivery, without prior authorization from Customer, and the procedures of Exhibit A shall control with respect thereto.

- d) Refused or undelivered shipments and the Carriers' obligations for the same shall be as set forth in Exhibit A.

#### PER DIEM - INTERMODAL ONLY

1. Charge for equipment utilization beyond free time when equipment is out of possession of railroad will be \$25 per day after the expiration of free time. Free time for Per Diem is fixed at both Origin and Destination.
2. At the Origin, free time is defined as the day the equipment is picked up from the rail plus two free days. The first weekend is free provided it falls within the free time. If the equipment is picked up on

 





Thursday or Friday, the weekend is free. If it is picked up on Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday, the weekend is not free.

3. At the Destination, free time defined as the notify day plus two free days. The first weekend is free provided it falls within the free time. If the equipment notifies on Thursday or Friday, the weekend is free. If the equipment notifies on Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday, the weekend is not free.

#### INTERMODAL STORAGE

1. Intermodal Storage - After the expiration of two (2) days free time, storage will be assessed at \$100.00 per day. Free time will follow the same schedule as Per Diem - Intermodal (Item 310)
