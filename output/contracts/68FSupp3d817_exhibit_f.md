## 3. Pre-Purchase Inspection

3.1. This Agreement is subject to Purchaser's pre-purchase inspection which includes a test flight satisfactory to purchaser and acceptable by seller (the "Pre-Purchase Inspection"). Purchaser or Purchaser's agent has contracted X-Air Flight Support, located in Hondo, Texas to begin the Pre-Purchase Inspection for Seller on or about January 15, 2008. The Pre-Purchase Inspection and the expenses associated with it (including fuel and reasonable pilot expenses related to the test flight) will be at the Purchaser's option and expense.

3.2. Prior to the pre-purchase inspection, the Seller shall provide copies of logbooks, engine covers and other Aircraft accessories for inspection. Seller shall deliver the Aircraft for the pre-purchase inspection with all systems, functioning normally and in compliance with all Airworthiness Directives and Mandatory Service Bulletins. Upon completion of Pre-Purchase Inspection by X-Air Flight Support, the Purchaser and Purchasers Agent (including pre and post test flight), a list of discrepancies shall be provided to Seller by the Purchaser or Purchaser's Agent. The Seller shall have 5 business days to review the list and to notify the Purchaser of Seller's decision to (a) pay to have the Airworthy discrepancies repaired at Seller's expense and to complete the sale; (b) reduce the Purchase Price of the Aircraft to Purchaser's satisfaction; or (c) to decline to pay the costs of defined Airworthy discrepancies and to terminate this Agreement.
