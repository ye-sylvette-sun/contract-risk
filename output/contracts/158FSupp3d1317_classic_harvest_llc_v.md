## **CONSENT INJUNCTION AND AGREED ORDER** **ESTABLISHING PACA CLAIMS PROCEDURE**

This matter came before the Court on the Motion of Plaintiff, Classic Harvest LLC (the “*Plaintiff*”) and for entry of an Order imposing a preliminary injunction and establishing a procedure for assessing the validity of all potential trust claims asserted against the Defendants, CRISP Holdings LLC d/b/a Fresh Roots (the “*Company*”), and its principals, Freshworks LLC, Fresh Roots LLC, CHS Holdings LLC, Joseph T. Cerniglia, Phillip Coleman, David R. Gattis, Jonathan S. Green, Timothy G. Harrison, Carl L. Perry, and Charles H. Schuchardt (collectively the “*Principals*”)(the Company and the Principals are collectively referred to as the “*Defendants*”), under the Perishable Agricultural Commodities

Act, 1930, *as amended*, 7 U.S.C. §§499a-499t (the “*PACA*”). Plaintiff and all other PACA trust beneficiary claimants before the Court hereinafter collectively (the “*PACA Claimants*”) (PACA Claimants and Defendants hereinafter referred to as the “*Parties*”).

WHEREAS, absent the Company’s consent to the relief requested herein, the PACA Claimants would have continued their active pursuit of permanent injunctive relief in this action.

WHEREAS, counsel for the Parties have conferred in an attempt to conserve fees and costs as well as the Court’s time in connection with this action, and have reached an agreement regarding the terms of this Consent Injunction and Agreed PACA Claims Procedure Order (the “*Order*”) and the manner in which the Company will satisfy certain claims against it entitled to trust status under PACA.

WHEREAS, the trust provisions of PACA establish a statutory, non-segregated trust under which Defendants are required to hold all trust assets including inventory, proceeds, and accounts receivable in trust for qualified beneficiaries until full payment is made. 7 U.S.C. §499e(c)(2).

WHEREAS, the Company has accepted service of Plaintiff’s complaint in the above styled civil action [D.E. #3].

Accordingly, **IT IS HEREBY STIPULATED AND AGREED**, by and between the PACA Trust Claimants and the Defendants, as follows:

A) The Company has indicated its consent to the entry of this Order and has waived personal service and any objection to the Court's exercise of both personal and subject matter jurisdiction in this matter in connection with the entry of this Order.

B) The Company does not have any objection to the entry of an injunction against it upon the terms reflected in this Order and any additional terms this Court deems necessary to give effect to such a request for issuance of this Order.

C) The Company recognizes that holders of PACA trust rights are entitled to a beneficial interest in the single pool of assets or PACA trust *res* which includes, without limitation, all inventory of perishable agricultural commodities ("*Produce*"), accounts receivable, proceeds from the sale of Produce, as well as any and all assets acquired or maintained with such proceeds, cash, assets and other trust assets held in the Company's name (the "*PACA Trust Assets*"). Qualified PACA trust beneficiaries presumptively have rights to the Company's other assets, including without limitation, furniture, fixtures, equipment, vehicles, leases, real property and leasehold improvements (the "*FF&E*"), all of which should be considered or deemed to be included within the definition of PACA Trust Assets. The PACA Claimants reserve the right to pursue their PACA trust claims, including without limitation all deficiency claims, if any, against any

person, entity, party or potential transferees of the PACA Trust Assets. Defendants, on the other hand, reserve all rights regarding the amount, validity and PACA trust status of the claims of the PACA Claimants and any other similarly situated PACA trust beneficiaries who properly join this action, as well as all legal and equitable defenses available with respect to the claims asserted in this case, including the right to seek to be removed from obligations contained in this Order.

D) The Parties agree this Order is reasonable and necessary to provide a procedural framework to review, qualify and satisfy any and all claims against the single PACA trust, to maximize the recovery for all unpaid beneficiaries of the PACA trust and to ensure the rights of all potential PACA claimants are efficiently addressed in a single proceeding without the expense of administering multiple separate actions to enforce the Defendants' obligations to all potential PACA trust claimants.

E) The Company hereby states and stipulates that its principal place of business is located at 2190 Brandon Trail, Alpharetta, GA 30004 and Freshworks LLC, is located at 5174 McGinnis Ferry Road, Suite 221, Alpharetta, Georgia 30005. The Company further states, and all parties agree, that the corpus of the PACA trust *res* is located within the State of Georgia.

F) This Order may be executed in any number of counterparts with the same effect as if all signatories had signed the same document. All counterparts must be construed together to constitute one instrument.

Based on the foregoing, **IT IS HEREBY ORDERED**, as follows:

### **I. CONSENT INJUNCTION**

1. The Court is satisfied that it has jurisdiction over this civil action as it arises under 7 U.S.C. § 499e(c)(5) and 7 U.S.C. § 499e(b)(2) of the PACA, pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1331. The Court has supplemental jurisdiction over Plaintiff's other claims pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1367(a). The Court has *in rem* jurisdiction over the Plaintiff's claims pursuant to, *inter alia*, 28 U.S.C. § 1655.

2. The Court is satisfied that venue in this District is appropriate, based on 28 U.S.C. § 1391(b), because a substantial part of the events or omissions giving rise to Plaintiff's claims occurred in this district and a substantial part of the property or trust *res* that is the subject of this action is situated in this District.

3. This Court shall exercise exclusive jurisdiction over the *res* comprising the Defendants' PACA Trust Assets. As such, all persons or entities having unsatisfied claims against the Defendants arising under or relating to PACA for unpaid deliveries of Produce to the Defendants shall have the right to seek a recovery against the PACA Trust Assets only in this action and only through the procedures established herein. Nothing in this Order shall be construed as a bar or

stay of any other litigation against the Defendants in other courts of competent jurisdictions that do not involve rights or claims with respect to the *res* of the PACA trust of Defendant Crisp Holdings, LLC or do not involve claims for restoration or liability for dissipation of such trust, all such actions being stayed by this Order.

4. The Company and any of its banking institutions are DIRECTED NOT to pay, withdraw, transfer, assign, or sell any and all existing PACA trust assets or otherwise dispose of any corporate assets to any creditors, persons, or entities until further order of this court.

5. Pending further orders of this Court, no banking institution (including, without limitation, Regions Bank, Arvest Bank, and BB&T) or other organization/entity (including, without limitation, Agrifact Capital LLC) holding funds for the Company shall pay, transfer, or permit assignment or withdrawal of any existing PACA trust assets held on behalf of the Company.

6. In furtherance of an accounting, the Court hereby directs Defendants to require any banking institution or other third party organization/entity holding funds for the Company to turn over to Counsel for the PACA Claimants any statements for the prior 120 days, signature cards, and all other documents relating to or regarding the Company's accounts, loans, or lines of credit at such banking institutions or other entities within five (5) days of receipt of this Order. The

Defendants have agreed and are hereby ORDERED to assist in effectuating the turnover of these documents if required by the bank or third party, as reasonably necessary.

7. Any and all injunctions as to the Principals shall be reduced and limited to prohibit only those extraordinary transfers that occur outside the ordinary course of their personal affairs. Principals' payment of their own attorneys' fees or those of the Company from non-PACA trust assets is expressly permitted. In all other respects, the injunction shall remain in place until further Order of this Court. This Consent Injunction supersedes any and all other temporary restraining orders ("*TRO*") entered against the Defendants to the extent of any inconsistencies and shall provide for the continued protection of all unpaid produce suppliers after the expiration of any relevant TRO.

8. The Company shall provide a detailed listing of all outstanding accounts receivable (the "*Receivables*"), accounts payable, and a detailed itemization of the assets and liabilities of the Company, as well as all other business assets to this Court and Counsel for the PACA Claimants (collectively "*PACA Counsel*") within five (5) calendar days of the entry of this Order.

9. Pursuant to Fed.R.Civ.P. 65, this Order will be binding upon the parties to this action, their officers, agents, employees, banks, or attorneys and all other persons or entities who receive actual notice of the entry of this Order. In

this regard, the Company is hereby directed to serve a copy of this Order on all financial institutions (i.e., banks, factors, insurance companies, etc.) with which the Company does any business or who may be holding any assets for or on behalf of the Company and to all unpaid suppliers of Produce to the Company. The Company, its agents, employees, officers, directors, representatives and any other person acting in concert with or on behalf of the Company, are restrained and enjoined from hypothecating, transferring or dissipating any PACA Trust Assets or withdrawing funds from the Company PACA Trust Account, as defined below, except as set forth in this Order or pursuant to further order of this Court.

10. For the purposes of this PACA Claims Procedure Order, the Court finds that the Company lacks sufficient cash or other assets from non PACA trust sources, to the extent such non-PACA trust assets are determined to exist, to finance either the administration of the PACA trust or the marshalling/collection of the Company's assets to reconstitute the PACA trust. The Court further finds that, to the extent any non-trust assets exist and are determined to be encumbered by UCC liens, such assets would be unavailable to administer the PACA trust or otherwise reconstitute the PACA trust for the benefit of the Company's PACA trust creditors. Accordingly, the Court further finds that it is necessary that PACA trust assets be utilized in order to enable the Company to administer such assets, including reasonable payment to employees to wind up the administration of

PACA trust assets, to hire counsel to carry out collections as required under this Order and review the validity of claims based on the records of Company and that utilization of PACA Trust Assets as allowed by this Order is in the best interest of the PACA trust and the PACA trust beneficiaries.

11. Due to the agreement reached between the PACA Claimants and Defendants, and in recognition of the fact the Defendants and Agrifact are currently in possession of 100% of the PACA Trust Assets at issue, the Court hereby finds good cause to set the bond in this matter at \$0.00.

12. The Company is hereby directed, within five (5) calendar days of the date of this Order, to provide a detailed listing of all cash, checks, deposits or other monies (collectively "*Funds*") in its possession, custody or control to the counsel for the PACA Claimants.

- (a) The Company is further directed to immediately transfer or cause to be transferred all funds in its possession, custody or control, to the Registry of this Court forthwith and shall continue depositing all PACA Trust Assets recovered, or otherwise required to be deposited hereunder, into this account, for the benefit of all qualified PACA trust beneficiaries.

- (b) The Company is further directed to immediately transfer or cause to be transferred all funds in the possession, custody or control of any bank or other financial institution or third party, to the Registry of this Court forthwith and shall continue depositing all PACA Trust Assets recovered, or otherwise required to be deposited hereunder, into this account, for the benefit of all qualified PACA trust beneficiaries.
- (c) The Clerk of the U.S. District Court and its Financial Administrator are hereby directed to accept any and all funds and deposit the same in an interest-bearing account associated with this civil action (the "*PACA Trust Account*").

The PACA Trust Account is for the benefit of all of the Company's PACA trust beneficiaries and is not subject to execution by any other creditor of the Company. However, Company, subject to the cap contained herein, is authorized to expend no more than \$7,000.00 dollars in September of 2015 and no more than \$1,000.00 per month thereafter as necessary to assist in the collection of PACA trust assets and assist in review of claims against the PACA trust. Company shall account to PACA Counsel monthly for the expenditures of all such sums.

14. 15. Agrifact is hereby authorized and DIRECTED to immediately provide to PACA Counsel and Defendants copies of all factoring agreements, UCC filings, loan files, account records, and statements related to any proposed or actual loans, disbursements, advances, or accounts of the Defendants within five (5) calendar days of the entry of this Order.

16. Counsel for the Company shall collect the Receivables and other assets of the Company, and preserve the funds so collected for the benefit of all creditors of the Defendants, as their respective interests may appear, by depositing them in the PACA Trust Account. Counsel for Company shall collect the Receivables and otherwise endeavor to liquidate the Company's assets and receive compensation as indicated below. The net recovery from any such receivable or liquidated asset, after deduction of allowed costs and the contingency fee, shall be deposited into the PACA Trust Account.

- c) Counsel for the Company is authorized to withhold a reserve from PACA Trust Assets for payment of its fees and expenses under this Order. In order to avoid the cost attendant to preparing and filing

multiple applications for attorneys' fees, Counsel for the Company shall submit and otherwise circulate its invoices no more often than quarterly to all counsel of record and shall file a Certificate of Service certifying the same. The Parties shall have ten (10) calendar days from the filing date of each relevant Certificate of Service to file and serve any objection to Counsel for the Company's specific fee request for services rendered under this Order. All objections shall be filed within 10 days of the date that counsel of record are provided with such applications or the objections shall be deemed to be forever waived. After the expiration of the 10 day objection period, and subject to Court approval, Counsel for the Company may file either an unopposed motion and proposed order for review by the Court and payment or a motion to resolve any timely filed objection to the same.

17. Before any settlement with any of the Company's qualified Receivables can be approved below 75% of the claim amount, Counsel for the Company will need to obtain the approval of all PACA Claimants or, if such consent is withheld, obtain Court approval. If Court approval is ultimately obtained for the proposed settlement, reasonable fees and costs of overcoming the

objection or withheld consent for the settlement shall be payable to Counsel for the Company from the party objecting or refusing to consent.

19. Within ten (10) days after entry of this Order, the Defendants shall cause any banking institution which is holding any funds belonging to the Company to deliver said funds to the undersigned Counsel for the Company for deposit into the PACA Trust Account.

29. Except for good cause and the absence of substantial prejudice to the non-moving parties shown, **ANY SUPPLIER OR CREDITOR WHO FAILS TO TIMELY FILE A PACA PROOF OF CLAIM WITH THE COURT SHALL BE BARRED** from thereafter asserting any claim under PACA against the Company, the PACA Trust Assets, other PACA Claimants, or third parties, including, but not limited to, the Company's Principals. While claimants may be represented by counsel of their choice in this matter, all Proofs of Claims shall be verified by either the owners or the authorized officers of the respective claimants having personal knowledge of the facts comprising such claims.

30. Documents that a PACA Claimant does not timely file and serve along with its PACA Proof of Claim or otherwise produce in response to discovery will be inadmissible as to such PACA Claimant at any evidentiary proceeding. This requirement does not apply to documents relied upon by a PACA Claimant to rebut a PACA claim objection.

31. Any objections to any PACA claims must be filed with the Clerk of the Court. Any and all such objections must be filed and served on or before the “Objection Deadline.” The Objection must set forth in detail all legal and factual grounds in support of for the objection.

32. On or before the “Response Deadline,” any PACA claimant whose claim is subject to an objection may file with the Court a detailed response to any objection received. The response may include rebuttal evidence that the responding party wishes the Court to consider, if any. A claim, or any portion subject to an objection, will be disallowed if a valid objection is timely filed and the claimant fails to file a timely response.

33. The claimant and the objecting party shall thereafter exercise best efforts to resolve any Objections. In the event the claimant and the objecting party are unable to resolve such dispute or the objection is not withdrawn, the parties shall submit such dispute to the Court for summary resolution, on or before the Deadline for Motion to Rule on Objection set forth in ¶ 26.

34. Any PACA claim listed on a Proof of Claim to which no objection has been filed and served prior to the Objection Deadline, shall be deemed a valid PACA trust claim for the full amount stated in the PACA Proof of Claim. Similarly, any PACA claim listed on a Proof of Claim to which an objection was timely filed but as to which an asserted PACA trust creditor fails to file a response

on or before the Response Deadline, shall be disallowed to the extent of the timely-filed Objection.

35. On or before the “Deadline to File PACA Trust Chart” set forth in ¶ 26, Counsel for the Company shall prepare, file with the Court, and serve upon all counsel of record that filed a PACA Proof of Claim a chart listing each person or entity having properly and timely filed a PACA Proof of Claim and a verified PACA Proof of Claim, the amount of the claims and the disputed and undisputed portions of each claim to expedite the Court’s resolution of the remaining disputed claims (the “*PACA Trust Chart*”). The PACA Trust Chart must also list the funds available for distribution on deposit at the time in the PACA Trust Account (“Available Funds”). The PACA Trust Chart must list the pro-rata distribution of the Available Funds to the undisputed and disputed claims, and allow for a reserve pending the overruling of valid objections. The PACA Trust Chart shall also identify any reserve amount for fees and expenses of Counsel for the Company under paragraph 16(b). Compensation for providing this service to all trust creditors shall be \$1,000.00 for fees, plus costs and professional fees and expenses for attending any hearings on or with respect to such PACA Trust Chart.

36. On or before the Deadline for Objecting to PACA Trust Chart set forth in ¶ 26, any party in interest may file and serve on counsel of record an objection to the PACA Trust Chart. If no objections to the proposed treatment of a

claim as listed on the PACA Trust Chart are filed, then all figures shall be conclusively determined. Any timely objections to the PACA Trust Chart shall be resolved between the parties or submitted to the Court on motion for ruling.

37. The mere objection to the characterization of a claim as disputed or undisputed will not serve as a valid objection to the PACA Trust Chart or prevent distribution according to its terms. In any event, Counsel for the Company shall file both a motion for approval of the Proposed Distribution and a draft order directing the Clerk of the Court to disburse the Available Funds in the PACA Trust Account to the holders of valid PACA trust claims in accordance with, and as set forth in, the PACA Trust Chart on or before the Deadline to File Motion to Resolve Objections to PACA Trust Chart and to Approve First Interim Distribution. The Clerk of the Court shall make the distributions payable to the client trust account of the claimant's attorney of record. If any attorney represents more than one claimant, the distribution may be made in one lump sum in the aggregate amount of all the attorney's clients' distributions. Each attorney has the duty to distribute the funds to their clients in accordance with the PACA Trust Chart. Compensation to Counsel for the Company for providing this service to all trust creditors shall be \$1,500.00 for fees, plus costs.

38. The pro-rata portion of any claim subject to an objection shall be held in the PACA Trust Account until such time as the objection to the particular

proposed distribution is resolved. Upon resolution of the objection, the reserve portion shall be used either to make the pro-rata distribution to the claim if the Claimant prevails or shall be made available for distribution to other valid claims, proportional to their valid amounts, if the objection is sustained.

39. At the time the PACA Trust Chart is filed, Counsel for the Company shall file and serve on all counsel of record who filed a PACA Proof of Claim, a report detailing all objections to claims and attaching thereto the PACA Proof of Claim subject to an objection, the objection to the claim, and the response to the objection (“*Report of Claims*”). Compensation to Counsel for the Company for providing this service to all trust creditors shall be \$1,500.00 for fees, plus costs. This Court shall exercise exclusive *in rem* jurisdiction over this action, the PACA Trust Assets and the PACA Trust Account established pursuant to this Order. The Court also hereby retains jurisdiction to enter further orders to enforce the terms of this PACA Claims Procedure.

None of the fee caps contained in this order is intended to include professional fees and expense related to any court appearances or hearings associated with the resolution of any objections to any such filings or responding to discovery. Because PACA Trust Assets are being used to administer the trust, telephonic appearances of counsel are permitted for all non-evidentiary hearings without further leave or order of Court.
