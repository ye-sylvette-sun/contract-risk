                         TIDS SECOND AMEN"DMENT TO DARK FIBER LEASE AGREEMENT ("Amendment"), is
                made and entered into this 9111 day of August, 2012, by and between TEXHOMA FIBER,. Ine., (hereinafter
                referred to as "TEXHOMA FIBER"), and PEG Bandwidth- TX, LLC, (hereinafter referred to as "PEG").

                          WHEREAS, TEXHOMA FIBER and PEG entered into a Dark Fiber Lease Agreement dated
                June lst, 2011, as amended effective August 15,2011 (as amended, "the Agreement"), under which PEG
                agreed to lease certain dark fibers from TEXHOIv1A FIBER to a specific number of site locations
                identified in Exhibit A to the Dark Fiber Lease Agreement, for a term certain; and

                        WHEREAS, TEXHOMA FIBER and PEG desire to further amend the Agreement in order to
                reduce the number of site locations subject to the Agreement and to set forth certain other matters as
                agreed by the parties below;

                         NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants herein contained
                and contained in the Dark Fiber Lease Agreement, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt
                and sufficiency of which arc hereby acknowledged, the Parties mutually agree as follows:

                          1.       Timely Completion of Sites. The parties agree to further amend the list of sites subject to
                the Agreement in order to establish required completion dates for the sites listed on Exhibit A of this
                Second Amendment. The parties agree that time is of the essence with regard to the completion of each
                site by its respective "Completion Date" subject to Force Majeure events.

                         2.      Compensation; Updated Site List Notwithstanding Section 4.1 of the Dark Fiber Lease
                Agreement, the Site Fee for the five sites listed on Exhibit A to this Second Amendment shall be
                S60,000.00 (Sixty Thousand and No Dollars) per site. PEG shall pay the Site Fee in this paragraph for
                each of the five sites listed on Exhibit A hereto within 15 days oiPEG's Acceptance of each site pursuant
                to Article 5 of the Agreement. The sites listed on Exhibit B hereto shall no longer be subject to the
                Agreement, as amended. The parties each acknowledge that PEG has paid all mounts due under the
                Agreement, as previously amended, for all sites constructed by TEXHOMA FIBER and Accepted hy
                PEG as of the date of this Second Amendment.

                         3.        Increase in Number of Fiber Strands. The number of strands of fiber subject to the Dark
                Fiber Lease granted to PEG by TEXH01v1A FIBER (including both "Backbone" and "Lateral" fibers)
                depicted on the revised route map attached hereto as Exhibit C is hereby increased to six (6) throughout
                the fiber route ..

                         4.       Failure to Perform Required Maintenance. Following any notification from TEXHOMA
                FIBER to PEG that TEXHOMA FIBER is unable to provide maintenance required by Article 6 (other
                than as a result of Foree Majeure as defined in Article 11), or in the event that TEXHOll1A FIBER fails to
                commence Maintenance within four hours of notification by PEG that Stich Maintenance is required, or in
                the event that the Lease Fiber Optic Specifications are not met for 24 or more continuous hours, then PEG
                may, at its election and in addition to any other remedies available to it under this Agreement and
                applicable law, (a) perform the required Maintenance itself or through a third party. in which case
                TEXHOMA FIBER shall reimburse PEG for TEXHOMA's pro rata share of the cost of such
                Maintenance, which such share of the costs shall be equal to the percentage of fibers on such maintained
                fiber route(s) not leased to PEG, and/or (b) terminate the Agreement.
